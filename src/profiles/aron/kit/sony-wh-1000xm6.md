## What to look for

After a shift spent surrounded by noise, a pair of noise-cancelling headphones is one of the quickest ways to switch off, whether that's on the bus home, on a flight or on the sofa.

The noise cancelling itself matters most: how well it removes the low rumble of engines and traffic and the chatter of a busy carriage. Then battery life, so you're not charging every day. Comfort, because you might wear them for hours. And portability: headphones that fold take up far less room in a bag. Good microphones help too if you take calls on the move.

## Why this one

The WH-1000XM6 is Sony's current flagship, released in 2025. It uses a new noise-cancelling processor, the QN3, working with twelve microphones, and reviewers rank its noise cancelling among the best available.

Sony rates the battery at up to 30 hours with noise cancelling on and 40 with it off, and a three-minute charge from a Power Delivery charger gives about three hours of listening. At about 250 g they're light for their size, the padding is widely praised for comfort, and unlike the previous model they fold flat, so they pack neatly into a bag.

## Who it suits

Daily commuters on buses and trains, anyone who flies regularly, and people who want real quiet after a loud working day without leaving the house.

## Who should skip it

If you want Sony's noise cancelling for less, the previous WH-1000XM5 is still excellent and appears on this site too. If you mainly want something for the gym or a run, sports earbuds are a better fit. And if you need to plug into an in-flight system or a computer over USB-C for audio, these won't do it.
