## What to look for

On a long shift you can walk a surprising distance without leaving the building. A fitness band shows how far that really is, how well you sleep afterwards and how your resting heart rate changes over the weeks, without the bulk or the nightly charging of a full smartwatch.

The screen matters most: it has to be readable at a glance, including in bright sun. Then battery life, because a band that needs charging every night soon ends up in a drawer. It should be light enough to forget about, water-resistant enough for rain and swimming, and it should work with the phone you already have.

## Why this one

The Smart Band 10 is Xiaomi's 2025 band, the latest in a line that reviewers have long recommended as the best-value fitness tracker. It has a 1.72-inch AMOLED screen that reaches 1,500 nits, bright enough to read outdoors, in a body that weighs about 16 g without the strap.

Xiaomi rates the battery at up to 21 days of typical use, and a full charge takes about an hour. The band tracks steps, heart rate, blood oxygen, stress and sleep around the clock, has more than 150 sport modes, and is rated 5 ATM, so it can be worn for swimming in a pool. It shows notifications from your phone and works with both Android and iPhone through the Mi Fitness app. This listing is the black version.

## Who it suits

People who spend all day on their feet and want to know how far they actually walk, anyone keeping an eye on their sleep after late shifts, and those who want notifications on the wrist without a smartwatch that needs charging every day.

## Who should skip it

Runners and cyclists who want to leave the phone at home need a watch with built-in GPS. If you want to pay by tapping your wrist, look for a model with NFC. And if you already wear a smartwatch, this adds little.
