## What to look for

A bag for the journey to a physical job has to carry two very different things: a laptop that needs protecting and a change of clothes that needs space.

A padded laptop sleeve that sits off the bottom of the bag keeps the computer from taking the knock when you put the bag down. Water-resistant fabric, and ideally a closure that sheds rain, keeps both dry on a wet walk or a scooter ride. Padded, ventilated shoulder straps and back panel make a difference in a warm climate. And 25 to 30 litres is roughly the size that takes a laptop, clothes and lunch without looking like a hiking pack.

## Why this one

The Thule Paramount 27L is made from 420D nylon with a water-resistant finish, and it closes with a fold-top held by a magnetic flap, so there is no zip across the top for rain to find.

The padded laptop compartment takes machines up to 16 inches and has its own zip on the back panel. You can take the laptop out without opening the main section, and in crowds it stays against your back. The fold-top expands when you need room for clothes or shoes, there is a padded tablet sleeve and a file slot inside, and the stretchy side pockets hold a large water bottle. The back panel and straps are padded for daily wear.

## Who it suits

People who cycle, walk or take the bus to work with a laptop and a spare shirt, and anyone who wants one tidy-looking bag that also works as a carry-on for a weekend away.

## Who should skip it

If you carry heavy loads over long distances, a pack with a hip belt will be more comfortable. If you want to reach everything instantly, a zip-top backpack is quicker. And if you have narrow or very broad shoulders, try it on if you can, since strap width is where opinions differ most.
