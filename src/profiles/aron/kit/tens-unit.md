## What it is

A TENS unit is a small battery-powered device that sends a mild electrical current through sticky pads placed on the skin. You control the intensity and the pattern, and most units run on a timer. Manufacturers describe the sensation as tingling or buzzing, and sell them for personal comfort at home.

## What to look for

If you're choosing one, these are the things that matter day to day:

- **Rechargeable, not disposable batteries.** A unit you use most evenings gets through AA batteries quickly.
- **Pads you can buy again easily.** The gel pads lose their stick with use, so check that replacements for your model are easy to find on Amazon.es.
- **Simple controls.** After a ten-hour shift you want a dial or two buttons, not a menu.
- **A timer that switches it off.** Useful if you use it on the sofa at the end of the day.

## Who should skip it

Anyone the "Before you buy" note at the top of this page applies to, and anyone who doesn't like the sensation of the current. If you're not sure whether a TENS unit is suitable for you, ask your doctor or pharmacist before buying one.
