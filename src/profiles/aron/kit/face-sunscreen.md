## What to look for

Skin on the face behaves differently from the rest of the body. A sunscreen that feels fine on the arms can feel heavy on the face, look shiny by mid-afternoon or block pores. For daily wear on the face, look for a light texture that absorbs quickly, no fragrance, and the same high protection you would want anywhere else: SPF 50 and a strong UVA rating.

## Why this one

Altruist's Face Fluid comes from the same dermatologist-founded brand as its body sunscreen and offers the same protection: SPF 50 with a UVA rating of PPD 52, far above the EU minimum. The difference is the texture. It is a thin, almost watery fluid that users say sinks in within about a minute and leaves little shine, which suits oily and combination skin.

The maker adds niacinamide, a form of vitamin B3 that it says supports the skin barrier, and vitamin E as an antioxidant. It is fragrance-free and non-comedogenic, meaning it is formulated not to block pores.

## Who it suits

People who want a separate, lighter sunscreen for the face on hot days behind a counter, on a terrace or on the commute, particularly those with oily or blemish-prone skin who find richer creams uncomfortable in the heat.

## Who should skip it

If your skin is dry, the richer SPF 50 cream from the same brand will feel more comfortable and covers face and body in one bottle. If you want a tinted or matte finish, look elsewhere. And as with any sunscreen, shade, a hat and reapplying through the day matter as much as the bottle you choose.
