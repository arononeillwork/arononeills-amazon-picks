## What to look for

After ten hours on your feet, the fewer pans involved in dinner, the better. A countertop multi-cooker earns its space if it can cook a whole meal at once, from fresh or frozen, without you standing over it, and if the washing up is one pan.

Look for a capacity that suits the number of people you feed, functions you will genuinely use, such as steaming, air frying, baking and slow cooking, and trays and pans that go in the dishwasher.

## Why this one

Ninja's Combi cooks with steam and hot air together. Water in the bottom of the pan turns to steam and cooks rice, pasta or a sauce underneath, while the air-fry element above crisps meat, fish or vegetables on a tray. Ninja says a family-sized meal can be ready in about 15 minutes this way.

It has 12 functions: combi meals, combi crisp, combi bake, rice and pasta, sear and sauté, steam, slow cook, prove, bake, reheat, air fry and grill. The pan holds 12.5 litres, enough to feed a family, and the box includes a baking tray, the combi pan, a crisper grid and a recipe guide. The trays are dishwasher-safe, and the manual explains how to run the steam function to loosen baked-on mess inside.

## Who it suits

People who get home late and want a hot meal with the least possible effort, households cooking for several people, and flats where the oven is small or slow to heat.

## Who should skip it

If you mostly cook for one, a simple air fryer is smaller, quicker to clean and does many of the same jobs. If your worktop is short on space or sits under low wall cupboards, check the measurements and the room the door needs before ordering. And if you enjoy cooking on the hob, this is a big appliance for things your pans already do.
