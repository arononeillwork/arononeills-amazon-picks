## What to look for

If you work on a terrace, walk or ride to work, or spend days off outside, sunscreen belongs in the kit. Two numbers matter. SPF measures protection from UVB, the rays behind sunburn, and SPF 50 sits in the high band on EU labels, just below 50+. UVA protection is rated separately, and EU rules only ask for at least a third of the SPF, so a higher UVA figure is better.

Beyond the numbers, the best sunscreen is the one you will actually wear every day: a texture you don't mind, no fragrance if your skin is sensitive, and water resistance for sweaty shifts.

## Why this one

Altruist was founded by a UK consultant dermatologist and skin cancer surgeon, Dr Andrew Birnie. This SPF 50 pairs its UVB rating with a UVA rating of PPD 52, far above the EU minimum, using a broad mix of photostable filters.

The maker describes it as fragrance-free, hypoallergenic and non-comedogenic, meaning it is formulated not to block pores. The unscented, water-resistant formula rubs in to a moisturising finish that doesn't feel greasy once it has absorbed, and the same bottle works on face and body, which keeps things simple if you only want one product in your bag.

## Who it suits

Anyone who works or commutes outdoors in the Andalusian sun, people with sensitive skin who avoid perfumed products, and families who want one high-protection bottle for everyone.

## Who should skip it

If you have oily skin and want a matte finish, a lighter fluid, such as Altruist's own face fluid, will feel better on hot days. If you will be in and out of the sea all day, reapply after every swim whatever the label says. And sunscreen is one layer of protection: shade, a hat and covering up still matter in the middle of the day.
