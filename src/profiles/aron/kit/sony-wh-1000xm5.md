## What to look for

Noise-cancelling headphones make a long commute or a flight far more restful, and they are a simple way to get some quiet after a loud day at work.

The things that matter are the strength of the noise cancelling against engines, traffic and voices, battery life for days away from a charger, and comfort for long sessions. If you don't need the very latest model, the previous generation of a good line is often the best value: most of the performance, with a few newer features missing.

## Why this one

The WH-1000XM5 was Sony's flagship from 2022 until the XM6 replaced it, and it is still widely recommended for its noise cancelling and sound. Sony rates the battery at up to 30 hours with noise cancelling on and 40 with it off.

At about 250 g with soft, synthetic-leather padding, they sit lightly for hours. The design is sleek and minimal, the noise cancelling handles planes, trains and busy streets well, and they pair easily with phones and laptops over Bluetooth, with a companion app for adjusting the sound.

## Who it suits

Commuters and travellers who want proven, top-tier noise cancelling and don't need the newest version, and anyone who wants quiet at home after a noisy shift.

## Who should skip it

If you need headphones that fold small into a bag, the newer WH-1000XM6 folds and this model doesn't. If you want the very best noise cancelling Sony makes, the XM6 edges ahead. And for workouts, sweat-resistant sports earbuds are the better tool.
